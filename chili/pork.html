🌶️ Smokey Pork Chili Cook

Mesquite-smoked pork chili with red beans (pinto, black & dark kidney), finished in the crock pot. On the spicy side, obviously.

Planning — cook date lands after the wedding

    3Propane & smoke

    Fuel plan: one full 20 lb tank + one spare for insurance.
    The ~7-hour smoke at 250°F uses roughly a third of a full tank (a 20 lb tank holds ~430,000 BTU; the smoker draws ~15–20k BTU/hr → ~21–28 hours per tank). Mesquite chips or chunks go in the smoker's chip box.

    4Heat notes

    • Bloom the hot chili powder in the oil with the celery for 45–60 seconds before any liquid goes in — that's seasoned heat, not just hot.
    • Keep the jalapeño seeds in. All four of them.
    • Salt to taste at the very end, after the beans have had their hour.
