🍖 Lamb Chili with Mushrooms

Dried pasilla peppers, roasted garlic, and a trio of mushrooms over browned lamb shoulder. Deep, rich, and as spicy as you make it.

Stovetop cook — no smoker needed

1Cook timeline

    2Shopping list

    3Notes

    • Pasillas bring deep, raisiny chile flavor but mild heat — the hot sauce is where the fire comes from, so dose it to taste.
    • Masa harina thickens the sauce and adds a corn undertone; all-purpose flour works in a pinch.
    • Good garnishes: goat cheese, chives, fried or baked tortilla strips, chopped cilantro.
    • No epazote on hand? Cilantro covers the job.
